Priya’s future-state journey map

How human-led curation turns 15,000 titles into something Priya actually wants to watch, and brings her back from the DVD shelf.

Priya, the overwhelmed browser

Role: 34-year-old heavy viewer and 14-year subscriber.

Goal: Find something she genuinely wants to watch without having to hunt for it.

“I open the app, scroll for like twenty minutes, and close it without watching anything. There’s 15,000 titles but nothing I actually want. I ended up going back to a DVD.” UXR-01

Initiative

Strategy: Restore brand authority and win back users by prioritizing human-led curation and restoration over mass-market scale.

Value proposition: Transform 15,000 titles into a curated entertainment experience through personalised, high-quality recommendations that help users discover what they love, faster.

Emotional arc

Today: frustration and DVD retreat. Future: trust and return.
Today: frustrated Hopeful Understood Confident Satisfied

Four stages

1. Arrive

A curated “Tonight” pick

User action

Opens app to one curated “Tonight” pick → skips the twenty-minute scroll.

Internal state

Feels hopeful but wary → a human-chosen start earns another chance.

Pain point addressed

Replaces aimless scrolling → the first session ends with something chosen.

UXR-01

2. Narrow

Browse by mood

User action

Picks a mood like “quiet Sunday” → sees a short, relevant shelf.

Internal state

Feels understood → stops second-guessing every thumbnail.

Pain point addressed

Adds mood browsing and descriptive search → ends choice overload.

UXR-09 · BUG-1080

3. Trust

A curator’s note

User action

Reads a curator’s note on a hidden gem → gains confidence to press play.

Internal state

Feels someone with taste chose this → algorithm distrust fades.

Pain point addressed

Replaces franchise-clone rows → picks feel varied and personal.

UXR-02 · BUG-1091

4. Watch

Resume anywhere

User action

Watches within minutes, resumes on any device → finishes what she starts.

Internal state

Feels her evening was well spent → the app is her first stop again.

Pain point addressed

Eliminates zero-watch sessions → the DVD retreat loses its reason.

UXR-01 · BUG-1061
Dependency: Stage 4 holds only if resume-playback sync (BUG-1061) is fixed. A restart at 0:00 on another device could undo the trust built in Stages 1 to 3.

Advantages over the DVD workaround

Fresh discovery

Surfaces new curated gems regularly → her “shelf” never goes stale.

Instant, portable access

One tap on any device → no discs, no hunting.

Human taste at catalog scale

Curators vet 15,000 titles → breadth with a shelf’s confidence.
